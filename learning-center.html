Learning Center

Comprehensive STEM Learning Resources

Access all your learning materials, resources, and recordings from one central location. All resources are linked to Google Classroom and Google Drive for seamless learning.

📚 Lesson Notes

Comprehensive notes for all STEM subjects. Access theory, diagrams, and key concepts.

🧪 Practical Resources

Experiment guides, lab procedures, and practical activity instructions.

📐 Mathematics Resources

General and Further Mathematics study guides, formulas, and problem sets.

⚛️ Physics Resources

Physics concepts, problems, solutions, and experimental procedures.

🧬 Biology Resources

Biology diagrams, species classification, and life system information.

🧪 Chemistry Resources

Chemical equations, atomic structures, reactions, and lab techniques.

📝 Past Questions

Exam past questions and solutions. Prepare for WAEC, NECO, JAMB, GCSE, SAT, and more.

🎥 Class Recordings

Access recorded sessions from Google Classroom. Review and learn at your own pace.

📂 Student Resources

Additional study materials, research papers, and supplementary content.

How to Use the Learning Center

Step 1: Access Your Classroom

Log into your regional Google Classroom using your classroom code to access all resources specific to your time zone and region.

Step 2: Browse Resources

Navigate through the resource categories - Lesson Notes, Practical Resources, Subject-Specific Materials, and Past Questions.

Step 3: Study Materials

Download or view materials directly from Google Drive. All files are organized by subject and topic.

Step 4: Review Recordings

Access recorded class sessions to review concepts, catch up on missed classes, or prepare for exams.

Step 5: Submit Work

Use Google Classroom to submit assignments and receive feedback from your instructors.

Step 6: Track Progress

Monitor your learning progress, grades, and upcoming assignments through the classroom dashboard.
