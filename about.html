About VIS Academy

Discover Our Story, Mission, and Vision

Who We Are

Velocity International STEM Academy (VIS-Academy) is an international online STEM academy dedicated to making Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics easier, practical and enjoyable for learners worldwide.

Since our establishment in 2018, we have been connecting students across continents through live, interactive classes that combine theory with practical experiments and real-world applications.

Our Mission

To inspire learners, develop problem-solving skills and build academic excellence through engaging STEM education.

Our Vision

To shape the next generation of scientists, engineers, innovators and technology leaders.

Our Commitment

At VIS Academy, we believe that quality STEM education should be accessible to every student, regardless of their geographic location. Our commitment is to provide:

  • ✓ World-class STEM education through live interactive classes
  • ✓ Experienced educators with 15+ years of teaching expertise
  • ✓ Practical, hands-on learning experiences
  • ✓ Comprehensive exam preparation for international standards
  • ✓ Flexible learning schedules for global time zones
  • ✓ Supportive learning community across 6 continents
