Who Can Apply?

📚 Students 10 years and above

Whether you are in JSS1 (Year 7), SS3 (Year 12), or any grade between, VIS Academy welcomes learners who are committed to mastering STEM subjects.

Requirements

✓ Interest in STEM

A genuine passion for Science, Technology, Engineering, or Mathematics

✓ Internet Connection

Reliable internet access for live classes and learning materials

✓ Device

A computer, laptop, or tablet for class participation

✓ Commitment

Dedication to attending classes and completing assignments

✓ Audio Setup

Headphones or speakers for clear communication during live classes

✓ Microphone

A working microphone for interactive participation (optional but recommended)
