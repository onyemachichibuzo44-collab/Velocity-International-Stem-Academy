# VIS Academy - International Online STEM Academy Website

## Website Structure

This is a complete, multi-page website for Velocity International STEM Academy (VIS Academy), an international online STEM education platform.

### Pages Included:

1. **index.html** - Homepage
   - Hero section with academy tagline and mission
   - Statistics about the academy
   - Student learning experience with beautiful gallery images
   - Why choose VIS Academy section
   - Call-to-action buttons

2. **about.html** - About VIS Academy
   - Academy history and background
   - Mission and vision statements
   - Commitment to students
   - Academy highlights and achievements

3. **courses.html** - Courses & Subjects
   - Science subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Basic Science)
   - Mathematics (General & Further Mathematics)
   - Technology & Engineering (Basic Tech, Technical Drawing, STEM Projects)
   - Learning approach section

4. **classrooms.html** - International Classrooms
   - Regional classroom codes for 6 continents
   - Nigeria, USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Europe
   - Unified Google Meet session information
   - Classroom features overview

5. **live-classes.html** - Live Classes Schedule
   - Fixed class schedule with timezone conversions
   - Monday: Mathematics + Basic Science
   - Wednesday: Physics + Chemistry
   - Friday: Biology + Past Question Marathon
   - Saturday: Bonus Revision (45 mins)
   - Class benefits and features

6. **learning-center.html** - Learning Resources
   - Lesson notes and study materials
   - Practical resources and lab guides
   - Subject-specific materials
   - Past questions and exam prep
   - Class recordings access
   - How to use the learning center guide

7. **exam-prep.html** - Exam Preparation
   - Exams covered: WAEC, NECO, JAMB, GCSE, SAT, AP, IB, and more
   - Exam preparation services
   - Past questions access
   - Revision classes
   - Exam Crash Course (₦20,000 / $75)
   - 1-on-1 tutoring ($20/hour)

8. **pricing.html** - Pricing Plans
   - Nigeria pricing (VIS Starter, Smart, Champion, Exam Crash, Family Plan)
   - International/Diaspora pricing (USD, GBP, CAD)
   - Special promotional offers
   - Installment plans, Pay 2 Get 3, Refer & Earn

9. **admissions.html** - Admissions
   - Eligibility criteria (10+ years old)
   - Requirements (internet, device, commitment)
   - 5-step admission process
   - Application form link

10. **payment.html** - Payment Methods
    - Wise (for UK, EU, USA)
    - Grey USD Account (for USA)
    - OPay (for Nigeria)
    - Geepay (coming soon)
    - Payment confirmation via WhatsApp

11. **faculty.html** - Faculty Page
    - Engr. Sir Velocity (Founder, 15+ years experience)
    - Sir Isaac (Physics & Basic Science specialist)
    - Teaching philosophy and methodology

12. **contact.html** - Contact Information
    - Email addresses
    - Phone/WhatsApp numbers for Nigeria and USA
    - Google Meet link
    - Social media links
    - Quick action buttons

### Getting Started:

1. Upload all HTML files to your web hosting
2. Ensure styles.css is in the same directory
3. Ensure script.js is in the same directory
4. Add images to an `assets/` folder:
   - logo.png
   - students-1.jpg through students-5.jpg
   - science-lab.jpg
   - math-class.jpg
   - And other science/education related images
5. Deploy and share the link
